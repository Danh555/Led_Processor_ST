# Master (ESP32-C3)

Du an nay la firmware cho ESP32-C3 dong vai tro master RS232, quan ly license qua WiFi AP/Web UI va giao tiep truc tiep voi thiet bi LedProcessor (EX Series) qua Serial1. He thong gom 2 khoi chinh: xu ly goi tin LED va quan ly license.

## Muc tieu va pham vi

- Quan ly trang thai license (mode, thoi gian) va luu NVS.
- Cung cap giao dien Web/WebSocket de cau hinh realtime.
- Ap mode license truc tiep len luong RS232 trong `LedProcessor`.
- Giam sat luong du lieu tu thiet bi EX Series, xu ly timeout va cac chung năng cua EX Series.
